# CastleCare Product Marketing Context

## Positioning

CastleCare is a premium local-services booking app for the jobs that keep a household moving: lawn care, laundry pickup, and window washing. The brand should feel trustworthy enough for a local Central Arkansas launch and scalable enough to grow into a managed marketplace.

## Primary Audience

- Busy homeowners and renters in Central Arkansas who want dependable help without calling around for quotes.
- Early customers who need confidence that a real local operator will follow through.
- Future repeat customers who can be moved into recurring care plans once the first job goes well.

## Launch Offer

- Start as a quote request.
- Capture contact details by step 2 so abandoned requests can be followed up.
- For lawn care and window washing, collect a $50 deposit to reserve the appointment, then invoice or collect the balance after service.
- For laundry-only bookings, collect full payment up front.

## Voice

Use confident, plainspoken copy. Avoid sounding like a giant anonymous marketplace before the operational network exists. The best tone is: polished local operator today, scalable service platform tomorrow.

## Core Messages

- Book home services like you book a ride.
- Clear quote flow before checkout.
- Vetted local care, status updates, and clean payment options.
- Built first for Central Arkansas.

## Active Services

- Groundskeeper Lawncare: mowing, edging, trimming, cleanup, and recurring care.
- Royal Wash Laundry: same-day wash and fold with pickup, delivery, and bedding options.
- Royal Pane Window Washing: exterior or inside-and-out glass care, screens, story count, and photo-assisted quote review.

## Current Conversion Goal

Move visitors from service interest to a saved quote request, then to payment. Do not require authentication to start booking. Account finalization can happen after checkout.
